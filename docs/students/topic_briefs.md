# 🧭 Topic Briefs — One-Page Framings for Each Marketing Category

> A one-page "so-what" framing for each of the 11 categories: what the topic is
> about, the questions it answers, where to start reading, and the gap/angle
> worth exploring. Use these as a scaffold for an essay, presentation, or
> thesis topic.

For every category, the **Start here** papers are the same verified,
high-quality picks from the [starter reading list](starter_reading_list.md).

---

## 🤖 AI & Marketing
**What it is:** how artificial intelligence — especially generative AI — is
reshaping marketing practice: content creation, personalization, lead scoring,
chatbots, and marketing automation.
**Key questions:** Can AI *create* marketing content and experiences as well as
humans? How does AI change the marketer's role and consumer trust? What are the
ethics and risks?
**Start here:** Grewal et al. *How generative AI Is shaping the future of
marketing* (2024) → *The power of generative marketing* (2024) → *Consumer
vulnerability in AI-driven marketing* (2026).
**Gap/angle:** the corpus has thousands of AI-marketing papers but very few that
connect AI to *contextual awareness* — see the [CAM framework](../../paper/g4_situational_awareness_framework.md).

## 📣 Digital & Performance Marketing
**What it is:** paid and performance channels — search, social, programmatic —
and how to attribute and measure their effectiveness.
**Key questions:** Which channels and channel combinations actually drive sales?
How do you measure performance without perfect data?
**Start here:** *Beyond the Pair: Media Archetypes and Complex Channel Synergies*
(2024) → *Different engagement dimensions, different outcomes* (2026).
**Gap/angle:** attribution and measurement dominate, but *experimental* evidence
on cross-channel synergy is thinner than the practical demand for it.

## 📱 Social Media & Creator Marketing
**What it is:** influencer marketing, the creator economy, social commerce, and
user-generated content.
**Key questions:** What makes an influencer (human or virtual) actually change
behavior? How do trust, authenticity, and community drive engagement?
**Start here:** *What drives virtual influencers' impact?* (2026) → *Virtually
human: anthropomorphism in virtual influencer marketing* (2024).
**Gap/angle:** virtual influencers and "de-influencing" (negative feedback) are
fast-growing, under-settled topics — good thesis material.

## ✍️ Content & Storytelling
**What it is:** content marketing, brand storytelling, and narrative strategy.
**Key questions:** How does content build brands and drive search/engagement?
Do stories actually persuade, and for whom?
**Start here:** *Brand-Neutral Content Marketing and Endogenous Search Prominence*
(2026) → *The storyteller matters* (2026).
**Gap/angle:** content marketing is analytically thin (few measurement papers) —
measurement of storytelling *effectiveness* is a real opening.

## 🧠 Consumer Behavior & Psychology
**What it is:** how consumers decide, feel, and act — purchase intention,
engagement, personalization, heuristics and biases.
**Key questions:** Why do consumers buy what they buy? How do experiences,
framing, and context shift decisions?
**Start here:** *Rating an Experience Makes People Notice How It Is Changing*
(2026) → *Sponsorship outcomes in sports rivalries* (2026).
**Gap/angle:** a huge, mature category — the white space is in *new* contexts
(virtual/AR, AI-mediated) rather than classic decision theory.

## 🏆 Brand Strategy & Management
**What it is:** brand equity, loyalty, positioning, extension, and brand purpose.
**Key questions:** What builds (and erodes) brand equity? When do extensions and
co-branding work?
**Start here:** *The foundations, evolution and promise of customer-based brand
equity* (2026) → *When Co-Branding breaks down* (2026).
**Gap/angle:** the CBBE review (2026) is a ready-made backbone; the interesting
open questions are in digital/AI-mediated brand building.

## 📈 Analytics, Attribution & Measurement
**What it is:** marketing analytics, media mix modeling, attribution, and
measurement methodology.
**Key questions:** How do you know which marketing worked? How do you attribute
and measure when data is incomplete or privacy-limited?
**Start here:** *Causal-driven attribution (CDA)* (2026) → *AI-enabled marketing
analytics for SMEs* (2026).
**Gap/angle:** privacy-first, no-user-level-data attribution is a frontier —
pair this with the *Data, Privacy & Cookieless* category below.

## 🔐 Data, Privacy & Cookieless Advertising
**What it is:** first-party data, cookieless/targeted advertising, and the
privacy–personalization trade-off.
**Key questions:** How do brands target effectively after cookies? What is the
right balance of personalization vs. consumer privacy?
**Start here:** *Customer data privacy & security as a firm strategy* (2026) →
*Personalization payoff* (2025) → *Decision-making in cookieless advertising*
(2026).
**Gap/angle:** `privacy-data` is one of the smaller categories — and it is
*rising*, so it is both under-served and timely.

## 🛒 Customer Experience, Omnichannel & Retail
**What it is:** omnichannel CX, the customer journey, retail media, and
experiential retail.
**Key questions:** How do channels work together to create seamless experience?
What drives loyalty and repurchase across channels?
**Start here:** *Examining the customer experience in the metaverse retail
revolution* (2024) → *One cart, many channels* (2026).
**Gap/angle:** metaverse/immersive retail CX is new and unsettled — a genuinely
open area with high novelty.

## 💼 B2B & Account-Based Marketing
**What it is:** business-to-business marketing, account-based marketing (ABM),
buyer journeys, and B2B contextual intelligence.
**Key questions:** How do B2B sellers reach and convert accounts? What context
matters at each stage of the buying journey?
**Start here:** *Unpacking account-based marketing* (2026) → *Sales funnel
structures in B2B digital platforms* (2025).
**Gap/angle:** the **fastest-growing** category with the **thinnest measurement
cell** (`b2b/measurement`) — a prime thesis spot. See the [B2B contextual
intelligence framework](../../paper/g6_b2b_contextual_intelligence.md).

## 📊 Surveys, Trends & Meta-Studies
**What it is:** big-picture evidence — literature reviews, meta-analyses,
bibliometric studies — the "meta" layer over all the above.
**Key questions:** What does the body of evidence actually say? Where is
research heading? What gaps repeat across studies?
**Start here:** *A meta-analysis of green marketing communication effectiveness*
(2026) → the corpus's own [literature review](../research/literature_review.md).
**Gap/angle:** the corpus's [research-gap deep dive](../../paper/gaps_deep_dive.md)
ranks the most promising under-served topics — start there for thesis ideas.

## How to turn a brief into work

1. Pick a category + an angle from the **Gap/angle** lines above.
2. Read the **Start here** papers (full text, not just abstracts).
3. Run the tools to broaden: `topic_planner`, `trend_scanner`,
   `brief_generator "your topic" --papers 8`.
4. Frame a question, then use the [browser](../index.html) (filter Review → then
   Empirical) to build your evidence base.
5. Cite the *original papers* — never this corpus — and always open the source
   before you cite it.

*Every URL in the starter reading list is real and verified from `papers.yaml`.*
